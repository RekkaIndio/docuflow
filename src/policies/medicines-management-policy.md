# Medicines Management Policy

**Policy Title:** Medicines Management  
**Effective Date:** [Insert Date]  
**Review Date:** [Insert Date – usually annually]  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Applies to:** All staff involved in the handling, administration, storage, and disposal of medicines at [INSERT CARE HOME NAME]

## 1. Policy Statement
At [INSERT CARE HOME NAME], we are committed to the safe, effective, and accountable management of medicines to promote the health and wellbeing of our residents.

This policy ensures compliance with:
- The Medicines Act 1968
- Health and Social Care Act 2008 (Regulated Activities) Regulations 2014
- Care Quality Commission (CQC) Fundamental Standards
- NHS and local prescribing and medicines management guidance

## 2. Purpose
This policy aims to:
- Ensure medicines are handled safely and correctly
- Protect residents’ rights to receive the correct medicines at the right time
- Minimise risks associated with medicines errors
- Comply with legal and professional standards

## 3. Scope
This policy applies to all staff who:
- Administer medicines
- Store and order medicines
- Record and report on medicines usage
- Dispose of medicines

## 4. Roles and Responsibilities
### Registered Manager:
- Ensures medicines management policy is implemented and monitored
- Ensures staff are competent and trained in medicines management
- Reports medicines-related incidents to CQC and relevant authorities

### Responsible Staff (e.g., Nurses, Care Staff):
- Administer medicines only after appropriate training and competency checks
- Maintain accurate records of medicines administration
- Report and document any errors or adverse reactions

## 5. Medicines Handling Procedures
### 5.1 Ordering and Receiving Medicines
- Medicines must be ordered only on valid prescriptions by authorised prescribers
- Check medicines received against the prescription and delivery note
- Report any discrepancies or damaged medicines immediately

### 5.2 Storage
- Store medicines securely in a locked cupboard or trolley with restricted access
- Maintain appropriate storage conditions (temperature monitoring for fridge items)
- Separate controlled drugs and maintain controlled drug registers

### 5.3 Administration
- Follow the “Five Rights” of medication administration:
  - Right resident
  - Right medicine
  - Right dose
  - Right time
  - Right route
- Confirm identity using at least two identifiers (e.g., name and date of birth)
- Ensure privacy and dignity during administration

### 5.4 Documentation
- Complete Medication Administration Records (MAR) accurately and promptly
- Record refusals, missed doses, and any adverse reactions
- Maintain confidentiality of medicines records

### 5.5 Disposal
- Dispose of unwanted, out-of-date, or unused medicines according to local protocols
- Controlled drugs must be disposed of in line with legal requirements and witnessed
- Maintain disposal records

## 6. Controlled Drugs (CDs)
- Maintain a controlled drugs register for all CDs received, administered, or disposed
- Only authorised staff may access CDs
- Conduct regular audits of CDs
- Report discrepancies or theft immediately to management and authorities

## 7. Resident Involvement
- Respect residents’ rights to be involved in decisions about their medicines
- Provide information and support about medicines as appropriate
- Record any allergies or adverse drug reactions

## 8. Training and Competency
- All staff involved in medicines management will receive training at induction and refresher courses annually
- Competency assessments are carried out regularly
- Records of training and competency must be maintained

## 9. Incident Reporting
- All medicines errors, near misses, and adverse events must be reported immediately
- Investigations will be conducted, and learning shared to improve practice
- Report serious incidents to the CQC as per regulations

## 10. Monitoring and Audit
- Regular audits of medicines storage, administration, and records will be conducted
- Action plans will be developed to address any areas of non-compliance
