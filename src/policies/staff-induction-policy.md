# Staff Induction Policy

Policy Title: Staff Induction

Effective Date: [Insert Date]

Review Date: [Insert Date – usually annually]

Version: 1.0

Applies to: All new employees, agency staff, and volunteers at [INSERT CARE HOME NAME]

## 1. Policy Statement

At [INSERT CARE HOME NAME], we are committed to providing a structured and supportive induction process for all new staff to ensure they feel welcomed, valued, and prepared for their role.

We aim to:

- Provide new staff with the knowledge, skills, and confidence to deliver safe, effective, and person-centred care.
- Ensure compliance with the Care Certificate Standards, the Health and Social Care Act 2008 (Regulated Activities) Regulations 2014, and CQC Fundamental Standards.
- Introduce new staff to our care home’s values, policies, and procedures.
- Support staff integration into the team and workplace culture.

## 2. Purpose

This policy sets out the framework for staff induction, ensuring all new employees:

- Understand their role and responsibilities.
- Receive mandatory training relevant to their duties.
- Are familiar with health, safety, and safeguarding practices.
- Are supported during their probationary period.

## 3. Scope

This policy applies to:

- All new permanent and temporary employees.
- Agency staff and volunteers (adjusted as appropriate to their role).
- Supervisors and managers responsible for delivering induction.

## 4. Roles and Responsibilities

**Registered Manager:**

- Oversees the induction programme and ensures compliance with regulatory requirements.
- Assigns mentors or supervisors to support new staff.
- Monitors completion of induction checklists and training.

**Supervisors / Mentors:**

- Provide day-to-day support and guidance to new staff.
- Ensure completion of induction tasks and Care Certificate requirements.
- Offer feedback and encourage reflective practice.

**New Staff:**

- Actively participate in the induction process.
- Complete all required training and assessments.
- Seek support when unsure about responsibilities or procedures.

## 5. Induction Programme

### 5.1 Pre-Employment Preparation

- Job offer letter, contract, and staff handbook issued.
- Pre-employment checks completed (DBS, references, right to work, etc.).

### 5.2 First Day Induction

- Welcome tour of the care home.
- Introduction to colleagues and residents.
- Overview of policies, procedures, and workplace values.
- Issuing of ID badge, uniforms, and access to systems.

### 5.3 Mandatory Training (within first weeks)

- Safeguarding Adults
- Infection Prevention & Control
- Fire Safety and Evacuation Procedures
- Moving & Handling
- Health & Safety
- Confidentiality & GDPR
- Equality & Diversity
- Basic Life Support / First Aid

### 5.4 Care Certificate (for Care Staff)

- All new care staff without prior qualifications will complete the Care Certificate within the probationary period.
- Evidence of learning will be recorded in a portfolio.

### 5.5 Ongoing Support

- Regular supervision meetings during probation.
- Feedback sessions with line manager/mentor.
- Review of progress against induction checklist.

## 6. Probationary Period

- Standard probationary period: [Insert duration, e.g., 6 months].
- Performance and progress monitored through supervision and reviews.
- Induction completion is a condition of successful probation.

## 7. Monitoring and Records

- Induction checklists completed and signed by staff and supervisor.
- Training records maintained in personnel files.
- Registered Manager audits induction records for compliance.
