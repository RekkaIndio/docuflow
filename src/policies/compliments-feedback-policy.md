1. Policy Statement
At [INSERT CARE HOME NAME], we value feedback from residents, families, visitors, and staff. Compliments, positive comments, and constructive suggestions are essential to help us:
	•	Recognise and celebrate good practice
	•	Continuously improve the quality of care and services
	•	Strengthen relationships with residents, families, and the wider community
We are committed to creating a culture where feedback is welcomed, recorded, acknowledged, and acted upon.

2. Purpose
The purpose of this policy is to:
	•	Provide clear guidance on how compliments and feedback are received, recorded, and responded to
	•	Ensure that feedback is used constructively to improve services
	•	Encourage openness and transparency in communication with residents and families

3. Scope
This policy applies to:
	•	All residents, relatives, visitors, and professionals providing feedback
	•	All staff who may receive or act upon compliments and suggestions
	•	All areas of the care home’s operations

4. Roles and Responsibilities
Registered Manager:
	•	Oversees the compliments and feedback process
	•	Ensures feedback is reviewed, recorded, and responded to appropriately
	•	Shares feedback with staff teams to encourage good practice
All Staff:
	•	Welcome feedback in a professional and positive manner
	•	Pass all compliments and feedback to their line manager or senior on duty
	•	Use feedback to reflect on and improve care practice
Residents, Families & Visitors:
	•	Encouraged to share compliments, positive feedback, or suggestions for improvement
	•	May provide feedback verbally, in writing, via surveys, or electronically

5. Feedback and Compliments Process
5.1 Receiving Feedback
	•	Feedback can be provided verbally, in writing, via email, through surveys, or by using feedback/comment cards available within the home.
	•	Staff must acknowledge feedback promptly and with appreciation.
5.2 Recording Feedback
	•	All compliments and feedback must be recorded in the Compliments & Feedback Log.
	•	Records must include the date, source of feedback, details, and action taken.
5.3 Acting on Feedback
	•	Positive feedback is shared with staff teams in meetings, newsletters, or handovers.
	•	Suggestions for improvement are reviewed by the management team and acted upon where appropriate.
	•	Where improvements are not possible, an explanation will be provided.
5.4 Sharing and Learning
	•	Compliments are used to highlight and reinforce best practice.
	•	Trends in feedback are monitored to identify areas for development.
	•	Feedback is reported as part of quality assurance and governance reviews.

6. Confidentiality
	•	Feedback will be handled sensitively and confidentially.
	•	Names will not be disclosed without permission, unless required for safeguarding or regulatory reporting.

7. Training and Awareness
	•	Staff will be trained to welcome and respond positively to feedback.
	•	Induction and refresher training will include how to log and share compliments.
	•	Feedback processes will be displayed and communicated to residents and families.

8. Monitoring and Review
	•	The Registered Manager will regularly review feedback records.
	•	The volume and nature of compliments and suggestions will be reported to senior management and used in quality assurance processes.
	•	This policy will be reviewed annually or sooner if required by regulation.
