1. Policy Statement
At [INSERT CARE HOME NAME], we are committed to supporting staff development and performance through a structured appraisal process.
We aim to:
	•	Recognise and celebrate staff achievements.
	•	Identify learning and development needs.
	•	Support career progression and professional growth.
	•	Ensure staff are equipped to deliver safe, effective, and person-centred care.
	•	Comply with the Health and Social Care Act 2008 (Regulated Activities) Regulations 2014, CQC Fundamental Standards, and best practice guidance.

2. Purpose
This policy sets out the framework for appraisals, which are designed to:
	•	Review performance and progress over the past year.
	•	Set objectives and goals for the next appraisal cycle.
	•	Identify training and development needs.
	•	Strengthen communication between staff and managers.
	•	Promote a culture of reflection, accountability, and improvement.

3. Scope
This policy applies to:
	•	All permanent and temporary staff employed at [INSERT CARE HOME NAME].
	•	Volunteers and agency staff may receive feedback through alternative arrangements but are not covered by the formal appraisal system.

4. Roles and Responsibilities
Registered Manager:
	•	Ensures an annual appraisal programme is in place for all staff.
	•	Monitors completion rates and quality of appraisals.
	•	Provides training and support for supervisors carrying out appraisals.
Line Managers / Supervisors:
	•	Conduct appraisals in line with this policy.
	•	Provide constructive feedback and support.
	•	Agree development plans and objectives with staff.
	•	Record outcomes of appraisals and ensure documentation is stored securely.
Staff:
	•	Actively participate in their appraisal.
	•	Reflect on achievements, challenges, and goals.
	•	Take responsibility for agreed objectives and personal development.
	•	Raise any concerns or suggestions during the appraisal process.

5. Appraisal Process
5.1 Frequency
	•	Formal appraisals will be held once every 12 months.
	•	A mid-year review or supervision session may be used to check progress.
5.2 Preparation
	•	Both the appraiser and staff member will receive a copy of the appraisal form in advance.
	•	Staff encouraged to prepare by reflecting on achievements, challenges, and goals.
5.3 Structure
The appraisal discussion will cover:
	•	Review of past performance and objectives.
	•	Feedback from residents, colleagues, and supervisors (where relevant).
	•	Strengths and areas for development.
	•	Training and career development needs.
	•	Objectives and action plan for the next 12 months.
5.4 Documentation
	•	All appraisal outcomes must be recorded on the official Appraisal Form.
	•	Both staff and appraiser must sign and date the document.
	•	Copies are stored securely in staff files, with access restricted to relevant management.

6. Monitoring and Quality Assurance
	•	The Registered Manager will review a sample of appraisal records to ensure consistency and fairness.
	•	Staff feedback on the appraisal process will be gathered periodically to identify improvements.
	•	Training needs identified in appraisals will feed into the home’s Training & Development Plan.

7. Confidentiality
	•	Appraisal discussions are confidential between the staff member and their manager, unless issues arise requiring escalation (e.g., safeguarding, performance concerns).
	•	Records will be stored securely in line with the Record Keeping / Confidentiality / GDPR Policy.
