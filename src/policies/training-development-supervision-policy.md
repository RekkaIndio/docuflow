## 1. Policy Statement

At [INSERT CARE HOME NAME], we are committed to providing high-quality training, supervision, and development opportunities for all staff to ensure safe, effective, and compassionate care for residents.

We aim to:

- Equip staff with the skills and knowledge required to carry out their roles confidently.
- Comply with statutory requirements including the Health and Social Care Act 2008 (Regulated Activities) Regulations 2014 and CQC Fundamental Standards.
- Provide structured and supportive supervision to promote professional growth and reflective practice.
- Foster a culture of continuous learning and improvement.

## 2. Purpose

This policy sets out the framework for:

- Induction and mandatory training.
- Ongoing professional development.
- Supervision and appraisal processes.
- Recording, monitoring, and reviewing staff learning needs.

## 3. Scope

This policy applies to:

- All employees, including care staff, administrative staff, and managers.
- Agency staff and volunteers (as appropriate to their role).
- Training providers and supervisors working with the care home.

## 4. Roles and Responsibilities

**Registered Manager:**

- Ensures all staff complete required training.
- Oversees the supervision and appraisal programme.
- Identifies and supports staff learning and development needs.
- Maintains training records and compliance monitoring.

**Supervisors / Line Managers:**

- Provide regular supervision sessions and annual appraisals.
- Support staff with personal development plans.
- Identify knowledge gaps and arrange training opportunities.
- Offer constructive feedback and guidance.

**Staff:**

- Attend mandatory and refresher training sessions.
- Participate actively in supervision and appraisal.
- Take responsibility for their own learning and development.
- Apply training and feedback to daily practice.

## 5. Training

### 5.1 Induction

- All new staff will complete the Care Certificate (if not already achieved).
- Induction will include training in:
  - Safeguarding Adults
  - Infection Prevention & Control
  - Fire Safety
  - Moving & Handling
  - Health & Safety
  - Confidentiality & GDPR
  - Equality & Diversity

### 5.2 Mandatory Training

- Refresher training provided in line with statutory and local requirements.
- Examples include:
  - Medication management
  - First aid / Basic life support
  - Food hygiene
  - Mental Capacity Act & DoLS
  - Dementia awareness
  - Person-centred care

### 5.3 Ongoing Professional Development

- Staff encouraged to undertake additional qualifications (e.g., NVQs, diplomas).
- External training opportunities supported where relevant.
- Training needs identified through supervision, appraisals, and audits.

## 6. Supervision

### 6.1 Purpose of Supervision

- Provide support, guidance, and accountability.
- Encourage reflection on practice and promote wellbeing.
- Identify training needs and career development opportunities.
- Ensure safe and effective care delivery.

### 6.2 Frequency

- Formal supervision provided at least every 6–8 weeks.
- Annual appraisal carried out for all staff.
- Additional ad-hoc supervision offered as required.

### 6.3 Format

- Supervision may be one-to-one or group-based (where appropriate).
- Sessions will be structured, documented, and confidential.
- Staff will be encouraged to raise concerns, reflect on practice, and set goals.

## 7. Monitoring and Records

- Training records maintained for all staff, including attendance and completion.
- Supervision and appraisal notes kept securely and confidentially.
- Training compliance monitored regularly and reported to management.
- Audits used to identify gaps and ensure continuous improvement.
