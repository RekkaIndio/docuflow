Health and Safety Policy

1. Introduction

This policy outlines the responsibilities and procedures to maintain a safe working environment and to comply with health and safety legislation.

2. Purpose

To ensure the health, safety, and welfare of staff, residents, visitors, and contractors by:

- Identifying hazards and assessing risks.
- Implementing control measures to reduce risks.
- Providing training and information to staff.

3. Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, volunteers, residents, and visitors at all premises operated by the organisation.

4. Responsibilities

- Management: Ensure compliance with health and safety legislation, provide resources, and monitor performance.
- Staff: Follow safe working practices, report hazards and incidents promptly.
- Health and Safety Officer: Coordinate risk assessments, training, and incident investigations.

5. Risk Assessment

Risk assessments will be conducted for all significant activities and reviewed regularly, or when there is a change to the work, equipment, or environment.

6. Training and Competence

All staff will receive appropriate induction and ongoing training relevant to their role. Records of training will be maintained.

7. Incident Reporting and Investigation

All accidents, incidents, near misses, and hazards must be reported immediately. Incidents will be investigated to identify root causes and prevent recurrence.

8. Emergency Procedures

Emergency plans will be in place for fire, medical emergencies, and other foreseeable incidents. Staff will receive training on emergency procedures and participate in drills.

9. Monitoring and Review

This policy will be reviewed at least annually or sooner if there are changes in legislation or following a significant incident.

10. Policy Approval

This policy is approved by the Board and is effective from the date of issue.
