1. Policy Statement
At [INSERT CARE HOME NAME], Closed-Circuit Television (CCTV) is used to support the safety and security of residents, staff, visitors, and property. CCTV may act as a deterrent to crime, assist in the investigation of incidents, and provide reassurance.
We recognise that the use of CCTV must balance security benefits with the rights of individuals under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018. CCTV will not be used to intrude upon residents’ privacy or replace staff supervision.

2. Purpose
The purpose of this policy is to:
	•	Ensure CCTV is operated fairly, lawfully, and transparently
	•	Protect residents, staff, and visitors from harm
	•	Safeguard property and assets
	•	Support investigations into security or safety incidents
	•	Ensure compliance with data protection legislation and CQC requirements

3. Scope
This policy applies to:
	•	All CCTV equipment and systems operated by [INSERT CARE HOME NAME]
	•	All recorded images and data captured
	•	All staff, contractors, visitors, and residents who may be recorded

4. Roles and Responsibilities
Registered Manager (Data Controller):
	•	Responsible for the management and lawful use of CCTV systems
	•	Ensures compliance with GDPR, Data Protection Act 2018, and ICO guidelines
	•	Authorises access to footage for investigations or external requests
Data Protection Officer (if applicable):
	•	Provides oversight of data protection compliance
	•	Advises on lawful processing and retention periods
All Staff:
	•	Must not tamper with or misuse CCTV systems
	•	Must report incidents requiring CCTV review to the Registered Manager
External Contractors (CCTV providers/maintenance):
	•	Must comply with data protection and confidentiality requirements

5. CCTV Coverage
	•	CCTV cameras will only be located in communal areas, entrances/exits, corridors, and external grounds where there is a clear security or safety need.
	•	CCTV will not be installed in private areas such as residents’ bedrooms, bathrooms, or staff changing facilities.
	•	Signage will be displayed clearly to inform individuals that CCTV is in operation.

6. Use of CCTV Footage
	•	CCTV recordings will be used solely for security, safety, and safeguarding purposes.
	•	Images may be used to investigate incidents such as theft, vandalism, abuse, or health and safety concerns.
	•	CCTV will not be used for staff performance monitoring except where an incident under investigation requires review.
	•	Requests for disclosure of footage will be considered case by case, in line with GDPR and safeguarding protocols.

7. Data Protection and Retention
	•	CCTV recordings are personal data and will be handled in line with GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.
	•	Footage will be stored securely, with access restricted to authorised personnel only.
	•	Retention periods will be [Insert period, e.g., 30 days] unless required for ongoing investigations.
	•	After the retention period, footage will be deleted securely.

8. Subject Access Requests (SARs)
	•	Individuals have the right to request access to personal data held about them, including CCTV images.
	•	Requests must be submitted in writing to the Registered Manager.
	•	Requests will be processed in line with GDPR within one month, unless exemptions apply (e.g., third-party data protection).

9. Safeguarding and Confidentiality
	•	CCTV may be used to support safeguarding investigations in partnership with local authorities, CQC, or the police.
	•	Confidentiality will be maintained at all times, and footage will only be shared on a strict need-to-know basis.

10. Monitoring and Review
	•	The Registered Manager will review the effectiveness of CCTV annually.
	•	Audits will ensure compliance with GDPR, data security, and safety outcomes.
	•	This policy will be updated in response to legislative or regulatory changes.
